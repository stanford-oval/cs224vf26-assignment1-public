### Clinical Phenotypic Spectrum and Cohort-Specific Frequencies

Karayol-Borroto-Haghshenas neurodevelopmental syndrome (OMIM #620985), also designated MSL2-associated neurodevelopmental syndrome (MANDS), is caused by heterozygous pathogenic variants in *MSL2* (R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMID:38815585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38815585)) F1. Clinical characterization across primary discovery and expansion cohorts demonstrates that core neurological and neurodevelopmental manifestations predominate over major structural malformations (R Karayol et al., 2024, [DOI:10.1016/j.ajhg.2024.05.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2024.05.004); X Lu et al., 2024, [DOI:10.1038/s41431-024-01576-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41431-024-01576-0)) F1, F3, F6.

### Discriminating Findings and Differential Diagnosis

Clinical differentiation of Karayol-Borroto-Haghshenas syndrome based solely on standard neurodevelopmental symptoms is challenging due to phenotypic overlap across Mendelian neurodevelopmental disorders F16. However, when contrasted against its closest biochemical and epigenetic counterparts within the male-specific lethal (MSL) chromatin-modifying complex, several distinctive patterns emerge:

1. **High Prevalence of Autism Spectrum Behaviors (~64%)**: Autistic traits and social-communication deficits occur in approximately 64.3% of individuals with *MSL2* mutations (R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMID:38815585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38815585); S Luo et al., 2026, [DOI:10.1007/s00018-026-06237-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00018-026-06237-7)) F6, F7. This behavioral prevalence is distinctly higher than in *MSL3*-related Basilicata-Akhtar syndrome (OMIM #300271; global patient count ~40–42) and *KAT8*-related disorders, providing a key neurobehavioral divergence (V Kulkarni et al., 2024, Cureus; Y Qing et al., 2025, [DOI:10.21037/tp-2025-227](https://doi.org/10.21037/tp-2025-227)) F2, F7.
2. **Qualitative Neuromotor Deficit Profile**: Motor impairment in *MSL2* is dominated by early feeding difficulties (81.3%), axial/truncal hypotonia (76.5%), and progressive motor incoordination or gait ataxia (11/25, 44%) rather than early-onset refractory epileptic encephalopathy or progressive hypertonic spasticity (R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMID:38815585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38815585)) F6, F8, F10. In contrast, severe *KAT8* mutations and severe presentations of Basilicata-Akhtar syndrome feature intractable seizures and spastic quadriplegia (Z Wang et al., 2026, iScience, [S2589-0042(26)02056-0](https://www.cell.com/iscience/fulltext/S2589-0042(26)02056-0)) F8.
3. **Confusable Chromatinopathies and Molecular Episignature Resolution**: The clinical presentation of *MSL2* exhibits non-specific features common across epigenetic conditions (e.g., global developmental delay, intellectual disability, hypotonia, and varied facial dysmorphism) (X Lu et al., 2024, [DOI:10.1038/s41431-024-01576-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41431-024-01576-0)) F16. Bedside phenotypic distinction alone between *MSL2* and allied epigenetic entities offers **low-to-moderate clinical confidence** F16. Definitive distinction relies on a high-dimensional peripheral blood DNA methylation episignature, which achieves near-perfect classification (Methylation Variant Pathogenicity [MVP] score approaching 1.0) against controls, *MSL3*-related Basilicata-Akhtar syndrome, and *KAT8*-related disorders (R Karayol et al., 2024, [DOI:10.1016/j.ajhg.2024.05.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2024.05.001)) F4.

### Negative Clinical Predictors (Findings Looked for and Absent)

Certain clinical and biochemical findings are systematically absent or exhibit low penetrance in *MSL2* probands, serving as negative clinical predictors:

- **Major Congenital Organ Malformations**: Congenital structural organ anomalies, including complex congenital heart defects and structural renal malformations, are absent in over 80% of probands (penetrance <=20%, <=5/25 in Karayol et al., 2024, [PMC11267526](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11267526)) F6. The presence of complex congenital heart disease, renal agenesis, or multi-organ dysgenesis strongly argues against an *MSL2* etiology and suggests alternative syndromic diagnoses F6.
- **Early-Onset Refractory Seizures**: Active seizures are present in only 36% (9/25) of probands and are generally secondary rather than presenting as early infantile epileptic encephalopathy (R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMID:38815585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38815585)) F8, F10. A presentation dominated by neonatal intractable epilepsy without preceding developmental hypotonia is atypical F8.
- **Loss of Global Bulk Histone H4K16 Acetylation**: At the cellular/biochemical level, loss of global H4K16ac is absent in *MSL2* syndrome (R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMC11267526](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11267526)) F2. While *MSL3* deficiency (Basilicata-Akhtar syndrome) and *KAT8* variants depress total bulk cellular H4K16ac, *MSL2* patient-derived iPSC lines maintain intact bulk H4K16ac levels F2. Thus, depleted bulk H4K16ac points away from *MSL2* toward *MSL3* or *KAT8* F2.

### Genotype-Phenotype Correlations and Demographic Influences

- **Variant Type and Haploinsufficiency**: Pathogenic *MSL2* variants comprise *de novo* protein-truncating variants (PTVs: nonsense, frameshift, essential splice-site) and domain-specific missense variants (R Karayol et al., 2024, [DOI:10.1016/j.ajhg.2024.05.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2024.05.004); X Lu et al., 2024, [DOI:10.1038/s41431-024-01576-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41431-024-01576-0)) F3, F5. PTVs act via haploinsufficiency, exhibiting phenotypic severity, penetrance of global developmental delay, hypotonia, and facial dysmorphisms equivalent to pathogenic missense variants F3. Seizures and motor incoordination are neither restricted to nor enriched in truncating alleles (R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMC11267526](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11267526)) F9.
- **Variant Position**: Missense variants cluster within functionally constrained regions, including the N-terminal RING finger domain (mediating histone ubiquitination) and the CXC/basic nucleic-acid-binding domains (R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMID:38815585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38815585)) F5. While domain-specific alterations may exhibit fine-scale epigenetic variations, severe phenotypic divergence across variant positions has not been documented F5.
- **Sex Discordance**: Pathogenic *MSL2* variants are autosomal dominant *de novo* mutations. There is no established sex-based discordance in presentation or clinical severity between male and female probands (R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMID:38815585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38815585); O Zgheib et al., 2026, [DOI:10.1111/cge.70071](https://doi.org/10.1111/cge.70071)) F5.
- **Age Evolution**: Early infancy is typically characterized by truncal hypotonia (76.5%), motor delay (88%), and severe feeding difficulties (81.3%), whereas speech delays (78%), gait ataxia/coordination deficits (44%), and behavioral manifestations (autism spectrum disorder, hyperactivity, anxiety in ~64%) become clinically apparent across early to middle childhood (OMIM #620985; R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMID:38815585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38815585); S Luo et al., 2026, [DOI:10.1007/s00018-026-06237-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00018-026-06237-7)) F6, F7, F8, F10.

### Contested and Contradictory Literature Findings

Several discrepancies and deprioritised hypotheses exist in published databases and reports:

1. **Hypotonia Frequency Curation Artifact**: The automated knowledgebase MalaCards records muscular hypotonia (HP:0001252) as "Very rare (1%)" in Karayol-Borroto-Haghshenas neurodevelopmental syndrome (MalaCards.org). This directly contradicts primary cohort reports by Karayol et al. (2024, [PMID:38815585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38815585)) and the OMIM entry (#620985), which establish that hypotonia is a core feature present in 18 of 25 individuals (72%–76.5%) F10. The 1% frequency represents an automated ontology mapping error rather than clinical evidence F10.
2. **Relative Proportions of Motor vs. Speech Delay**: An assertion in some clinical discussions suggested that speech delay occurs "out of proportion to motor delay" in >85%–90% of probands F18. In contrast, OMIM #620985 and Zgheib et al. (2026, [DOI:10.1111/cge.70071](https://doi.org/10.1111/cge.70071)) document motor delay in 21 of 25 individuals (84%–88%) and speech delay in 18 of 25 individuals (72%–78%), indicating that motor delay is at least as prevalent as, if not marginally higher than, expressive speech delay F10, F18.
3. **C-Terminal Variants Escaping Nonsense-Mediated Decay (NMD)**: It was hypothesized that PTVs escaping NMD in the terminal exon confer a distinct and more severe presentation featuring structural central nervous system defects such as ventriculomegaly (RI Torene et al., 2024, [DOI:10.1016/j.ajhg.2023.11.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2023.11.007)) F17. This was refuted by Zgheib et al. (2026, [DOI:10.1111/cge.70071](https://doi.org/10.1111/cge.70071)), who documented severe bilateral ventriculomegaly caused by an early 5' splice-donor variant (c.142+1G>T) leading to early intron retention and premature truncation, and associated with only mild neurodevelopmental delay at 11 months F17.
4. **Prenatal Sonographic Predictors**: Thickened nuchal translucency, cystic lymphangioma, and ventriculomegaly have been described in prenatal presentations (Q Xiang et al., 2026, [PMC13446537](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC13446537); O Zgheib et al., 2026, [DOI:10.1111/cge.70071](https://doi.org/10.1111/cge.70071)) F12. However, these sonographic abnormalities represent common, non-specific fetal findings across hundreds of genetic conditions and cannot reliably distinguish *MSL2* syndrome prior to molecular sequencing F12.
5. **Facial Dysmorphism Claims**: Hypotheses claiming that a rigid facial dysmorphism tetrad (arched/thick eyebrows, broad forehead, hypertelorism/telecanthus, and thin upper lip) occurs in 70%–90% of probands lack empirical verification in the literature block, as facial presentations remain subtle, variable, and non-stereotyped across unrelated probands (R Karayol et al., 2024, [DOI:10.1016/j.ajhg.2024.05.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2024.05.001)) F14.

### Synthesis and Open Questions

- **Mechanistic Divergence within the MSL Complex**: The preservation of global bulk H4K16ac histone marks in *MSL2* syndrome, in contrast to *MSL3* and *KAT8* disorders, highlights that MSL2's role spans chromatin functions beyond basic KAT8/MOF enzymatic complex recruitment, potentially operating through histone H2B mono-ubiquitination (H2BK34ub) or localized recruitment F2, F16.
- **Neural Circuit Vulnerability**: The sharp contrast between high penetrance of feeding difficulties (81.3%), motor incoordination (44%), and autism spectrum behaviors (64.3%), versus low penetrance of congenital structural organ anomalies (<=20%), underscores selective neurodevelopmental circuit vulnerability to *MSL2* dosage while gross embryologic organogenesis remains intact F6, F7, F8.
- **Diagnostic Pathway**: Given that clinical phenotypes alone overlap broadly with general neurodevelopmental delay cohorts, definitive identification of individuals with Karayol-Borroto-Haghshenas neurodevelopmental syndrome requires exome/genome sequencing corroborated by the highly specific peripheral blood DNA methylation episignature (MVP score ~1.0) (R Karayol et al., 2024, [PMID:38815585](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38815585)) F4, F16.
