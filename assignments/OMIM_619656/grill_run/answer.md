# Clinical Identification of Loeys-Dietz Syndrome Type 6 (SMAD2) by Phenotype Alone

## Executive Summary & Clinical Feasibility of Phenotypic Identification

Loeys-Dietz syndrome type 6 (LDS6; OMIM #619656) is an autosomal dominant connective tissue disorder caused by heterozygous pathogenic variants in *SMAD2*, which encodes an intracellular receptor-regulated effector within the canonical transforming growth factor-beta (TGF-β) signaling cascade. Identifying an individual with LDS6 from clinical phenotype alone within a cohort of patients exhibiting overlapping hereditary thoracic aortic aneurysms and dissections (HTAAD) or marfanoid habitus is clinically unfeasible with high confidence F1, F3, F18. Unlike canonical Loeys-Dietz syndrome types 1 and 2 (*TGFBR1*, *TGFBR2*), which frequently present with prominent craniofacial triads (hypertelorism, bifid uvula, and craniosynostosis), *SMAD2* pathogenic variants demonstrate striking phenotypic pleiotropy, reduced craniofacial penetrance, and clinical overlap with nonsyndromic thoracic aortic aneurysm and dissection (TAAD) F1, F3, F18.

While cardiovascular manifestations—predominantly thoracic aortic root and ascending aortic aneurysms—develop in 60% to 80% of clinically evaluated adult carriers F3, younger carriers frequently exhibit completely normal vascular calibers prior to progressive dilation F3. Furthermore, severe congenital heart disease (CHD) and heterotaxy have been identified in infants harboring truncating and missense *SMAD2* variants F1. Consequently, clinical diagnosis relies on high-index suspicion, systematic exclusion of hallmark features belonging to allied disorders (such as ectopia lentis in Marfan syndrome or craniosynostosis/intellectual disability in Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome), and mandatory confirmatory molecular genetic testing F2, F4, F9.

## Differential Diagnosis and Discriminating Features

When evaluating a patient with thoracic aortic aneurysm, skeletal features of connective tissue disease, and vascular tortuosity, differentiating LDS6 from phenotypically overlapping disorders relies heavily on relative discriminators and negative findings.

### Confused Disorders and Level of Diagnostic Confidence

1. **Marfan Syndrome (*FBN1*):**
   - *Confused Features:* Both conditions share ascending thoracic aortic aneurysm, tall stature, pectus excavatum/carinatum, scoliosis, and arachnodactyly F3, F7, F12.
   - *Discriminating Signs:* The presence of **ectopia lentis (HP:0001083)** is the cardinal hallmark of Marfan syndrome and is canonically absent in LDS6 F4, F7, F9. Arterial tortuosity (HP:0001053) affecting cervical, vertebral, and head/neck vessels strongly favors Loeys-Dietz syndrome spectrum over Marfan syndrome, although specific quantitative ranges for *SMAD2* remain unstratified F12.
   - *Confidence of Phenotypic Distinction:* **Moderate-to-High** if arterial tortuosity is marked or if ectopia lentis is present. However, if a patient presents solely with an isolated aortic root aneurysm and mild skeletal features, phenotypic distinction is impossible without molecular sequencing.

2. **Classic Loeys-Dietz Syndrome Types 1 and 2 (*TGFBR1*, *TGFBR2*):**
   - *Confused Features:* Progressive thoracic aortic aneurysm, diffuse peripheral arterial aneurysms, arterial tortuosity, hypertelorism F3, F12, F18.
   - *Discriminating Signs:* Canonical craniofacial features—specifically **bifid uvula (HP:0000193)** (documented in 55% to 62% of *TGFBR1* and *TGFBR2* carriers) and **craniosynostosis (HP:0001363)**—are frequent in LDS1/2 but absent or exceptionally rare in *SMAD2* carriers F2, F18. In specialized oro-dental and clinical evaluations, bifid uvula was documented in 0% of downstream *SMAD2/3* pathway carriers F18.
   - *Confidence of Phenotypic Distinction:* **Moderate.** The presence of severe craniosynostosis or a cleft/bifid uvula argues heavily for LDS1/2 rather than LDS6 F2, F18.

3. **Shprintzen-Goldberg Syndrome (*SKI*):**
   - *Confused Features:* Marfanoid habitus, thoracic aortic aneurysm, aortic root dilation, hypertelorism, camptodactyly F2.
   - *Discriminating Signs:* **Significant intellectual disability (HP:0001249)** and **craniosynostosis (HP:0001363)** are cardinal diagnostic criteria for Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome but are absent in typical adult-onset familial LDS6 F2.
   - *Confidence of Phenotypic Distinction:* **High.** Severe intellectual disability combined with craniosynostosis rules out typical *SMAD2*-related LDS6 F2.

4. **Vascular Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (*COL3A1*):**
   - *Confused Features:* Early spontaneous arterial dissection, rupture of hollow viscera, easy bruising, translucent skin.
   - *Discriminating Signs:* Aortic root aneurysm is characteristic of *SMAD2* (present in 66.7% of evaluated carriers, Peeters et al., 2019) F3, F6, whereas *COL3A1* vasculopathy typically causes dissections of medium-sized muscular arteries without marked antecedent root aneurysm dilation.
   - *Confidence of Phenotypic Distinction:* **Moderate.**

## Exclusionary Findings (Looked for and Found Absent)

In clinical genetics, features that are consistently absent in affected individuals serve as essential exclusionary criteria. For an individual suspected of harboring a *SMAD2* mutation, the presence of any of the following features argues strongly against LDS6:

1. **Ectopia Lentis (Lens Dislocation; HP:0001083):**
   - In foundational nosology and core clinical references (GeneReviews; Verstraeten et al., 2021, 10.1002/9781119432692.ch36; Dean et al., 2018, 10.1007/978-3-319-66114-8_19), ectopia lentis is completely absent (0% penetrance) across verified Loeys-Dietz syndrome types, including LDS6 F4, F5, F7, F9. Finding bilateral or unilateral lens subluxation points decisively toward *FBN1*-related Marfan syndrome or homocystinuria.
2. **Craniosynostosis (HP:0001363):**
   - Premature fusion of the cranial sutures is a hallmark of LDS1 (*TGFBR1*), LDS2 (*TGFBR2*), and Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome (*SKI*). It is absent or exceedingly rare in typical *SMAD2*-associated LDS6 families (MacCarrick et al., 2026, 10.1016/j.gim.2026.102577; Velchev et al., 2021, 10.1007/978-3-030-80614-9_11) F2.
3. **Moderate-to-Severe Intellectual Disability (HP:0001249):**
   - Cognitive development is typically normal in familial *SMAD2* cohorts (Velchev et al., 2021; Verstraeten et al., 2021) F2. Its presence indicates alternative diagnoses such as Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome (*SKI*) or broader chromosomal/syndromic microdeletion syndromes F2.

## Genotype-Phenotype Stratification: Variant Type, Domain Position, Age, and Sex

### 1. Variant Type (Truncating vs Missense)
Historical assumptions that heterozygous truncating (loss-of-function/haploinsufficiency) variants in *SMAD2* lead only to mild, late-onset isolated thoracic aortic disease have been refuted by clinical ascertainment F1, F13. Truncating, splice-site, and missense variants have all been documented in pediatric and infantile probands presenting with complex congenital heart defects, heterotaxy, developmental delay, and severe syndromic anomalies (Granadillo et al., 2018, 10.1002/humu.23627) F1. In functional cellular models, *SMAD2* haploinsufficiency directly dysregulated 385 downstream target genes, confirming that loss of one functional copy impairs embryonic vascular and left-right patterning programs (Ward et al., 2025, 10.1161/JAHA.124.036860) F1.

### 2. Variant Position (MH1 vs MH2 Domains)
Efforts to translate the domain-level genotype-phenotype models of *SMAD3* (where dominant-negative missense variants in the MH2 domain were hypothesized to cause earlier vascular events than haploinsufficient alleles; de Wagenaar et al., 2024) to *SMAD2* have failed F8, F10, F13. In clinical series, aortic dissections have been documented in carriers of truncating variants (e.g., c.593dupA) as well as MH2 missense variants (Yang et al., 2020, 10.1186/s13023-019-1282-3; Micha et al., 2015, 10.1002/humu.22854) F8, F10. Furthermore, large systematic studies in homologous *SMAD3* cohorts demonstrated no statistically significant correlation between variant type/position and aortic severity ($P = 0.935$; Chesneau et al., 2020, 10.1002/mgg3.1132) F10, F13. Thus, variant position within *SMAD2* does not reliably predict clinical severity.

### 3. Age-Dependent Divergence (Bimodal Presentation)
*SMAD2* displays a distinct bimodal presentation across life stages:
- **Perinatal / Pediatric Stage:** Manifests primarily as structural developmental anomalies, including complex congenital heart disease (tetralogy of Fallot, ventricular septal defects), heterotaxy, and branchial arch anomalies (Granadillo et al., 2018; Williams et al., 2019) F1.
- **Adult Stage:** Characterized by progressive, degenerative mechanotransduction failure manifesting as thoracic aortic root or ascending aortic dilation and risk of dissection (Peeters et al., 2019) F3, F6. Crucially, young pediatric carriers in adult-onset kindreds frequently exhibit completely normal aortic root dimensions on early echocardiography before progressive aneurysmal expansion in later decades F3.

### 4. Biological Sex
Published *SMAD2* cohorts do not demonstrate any statistically robust sex-specific difference in phenotypic penetrance, aortic dilation rate, or dissection incidence. Both males and females are vulnerable to progressive thoracic aortopathy F3, F6, F8.

## Explicit Literature Contradictions and Diagnostic Dilemmas

The medical literature on LDS6 and *SMAD2* harbors three prominent clinical contradictions:

### Contradiction 1: Complete Absence vs Documented Presence of Ectopia Lentis
- **Assertion A (Canonical Dogma):** Foundational clinical reviews and diagnostic guidelines state that ectopia lentis is strictly absent across all LDS subtypes (penetrance = 0%), serving as an absolute clinical counter-indicator that excludes LDS and confirms Marfan syndrome (GeneReviews; Verstraeten et al., 2021, 10.1002/9781119432692.ch36; Dean et al., 2018, 10.1007/978-3-319-66114-8_19) F4, F5, F7, F9.
- **Assertion B (Contradictory Literature):** Systematic meta-analyses and registry aggregations across 3,896 reported LDS cases document ectopia lentis in 2 out of 275 evaluated LDS individuals (Gouda, 2022, Int J Cardiol; Loomba et al., 2022; Nistri, 2021, 10.3390/genes12101462) F4, F5, F7, F9.
- **Clinical Resolution:** Most authorities consider these rare documented cases to represent phenocopies (such as severe myopia, microspherophakia, or secondary zonular laxity) or unverified clinical misattributions rather than true fibrillinopathy-like primary zonular rupture F5.

### Contradiction 2: Mandatory Aneurysm Dilation vs Aortic Dissection at Normal Calibers
- **Assertion A:** In the seminal delineation cohort of 15 *SMAD2* mutation carriers from five families, 10/15 (66.7%) exhibited thoracic aortic aneurysm, but acute aortic dissection was documented in 0/15 at ascertainment, suggesting dissection occurs secondary to progressive, detectable aneurysm formation (Peeters et al., 2019, BMJ) F6, F11.
- **Assertion B:** Authoritative guideline reviews and pathology analyses warn that TGF-β vasculopathies, including *SMAD2*, can suffer catastrophic acute aortic dissections at aortic dimensions below conventional surgical thresholds ($\ar{<}40$ mm or $\ar{<}45$ mm), challenging the reliance on preceding high-grade aneurysm dilation for surgical intervention (Meester et al., 2022, 10.1146/annurev-genom-111521-104455; Micha et al., 2015; GeneReviews) F6, F11.

### Contradiction 3: MH2 Domain Dominant-Negative vs Truncating Severity Models
- **Assertion A:** Missense mutations in the MH2 signaling domain were hypothesized to accelerate vascular disease progression and cause earlier major aortic events compared to truncating/haploinsufficient alleles (de Wagenaar et al., 2024) F8, F10, F13.
- **Assertion B:** Chesneau et al. (2020, 10.1002/mgg3.1132) explicitly proved a complete absence of correlation between MH2 missense variants and truncating variants ($P = 0.935$) in 155 patients. In *SMAD2*, truncating variants have been observed causing severe infantile presentations and early deaths (Granadillo et al., 2018; Yang et al., 2020), directly refuting a simple linear domain-severity model F8, F10, F13.

## Systematic Analysis of Deprioritised and Refuted Hypotheses

Several candidate clinical hypotheses were deprioritised or refuted based on rigorous evidentiary cross-examination:

### 1. Refuted Hypotheses
- **Arterial Tortuosity Frequency in 40–70% of SMAD2 Carriers [F12]:** Refuted. While arterial tortuosity is a recognized hallmark of generalized LDS cohorts (Velchev et al., 2021, 10.1007/978-3-030-80614-9_11; Gallo et al., 2014, 10.1172/jci69666), the literature provides zero empirical, SMAD2-specific imaging numerators and denominators to support the claimed 40–70% range F12.
- **Craniofacial Penetrance of 30–50% in Probands [F14]:** Refuted. No empirical cohort data, numerators, or denominators exist in the published literature to confirm that craniofacial features occur in 30–50% of *SMAD2* probands F14.
- **Proband vs Relative Craniofacial Disparity (>60% vs <25%) [F15]:** Refuted. Resolvable sources contain no systematic cohort comparison comparing craniofacial penetrance between index probands and cascade-tested relatives in *SMAD2* kindreds F15.
- **Overt Cleft Palate Penetrance <15% [F16]:** Refuted. Due to the extreme rarity of reported *SMAD2* probands, quantitative statistical confirmation that cleft palate penetrance is $<15\%$ is completely absent from the literature F16.
- **Phenotypic Dichotomy Driven by Nonsense-Mediated mRNA Decay (NMD) Escape [F17]:** Refuted. Molecular data demonstrate that probands presenting with severe congenital anomalies harbor missense variants as well as splice and truncating mutations, confirming that congenital malformations are not governed by an NMD-escape mechanism (Granadillo et al., 2018; Lin et al., 2020) F17.
- **Bifid Uvula Penetrance $\ar{\ge} 40\%$ [F18]:** Refuted. Specialized craniofacial cohorts demonstrate that bifid uvula is concentrated in LDS1 and LDS2, with 0 documented occurrences in *SMAD2* series (Camerota et al., 2019; Jani et al., 2020; Koefoed et al., 2025) F18.

### 2. Contested / Deprioritised Hypotheses
- **Universal 0% Penetrance of Ectopia Lentis [F4, F5, F7, F9]:** Contested due to the conflict between dogmatic exclusionary criteria and systematic reviews reporting 2/275 LDS patients with lens dislocation F4, F5, F7, F9.
- **Dissection Secondary Only to Antecedent High-Grade Dilation [F6, F11]:** Contested due to documented dissections at normal or near-normal aortic calibers in TGF-β vasculopathies F6, F11.
- **MH2 Domain Missense Severity and Earlier Vascular Dissection [F8, F10, F13]:** Contested due to contradictory cohort analyses ($P = 0.935$, Chesneau et al., 2020) and observations of severe vascular disease in truncating variant carriers F8, F10, F13.

## Synthesis: Cross-Cutting Patterns and Clinical Recommendations

### 1. Bimodal Presentation Across Life Stages
*SMAD2* pathogenic variants diverge developmentally: critical embryonic dosage thresholds during left-right patterning and cardiac looping manifest as congenital heart disease and heterotaxy perinatally, whereas progressive post-developmental mechanotransduction impairment leads to adult-onset thoracic aortic aneurysms F1, F3. Clinicians must recognize that an adolescent or young adult with a normal aortic diameter may still carry a pathogenic *SMAD2* variant and remain at risk for late-onset aneurysmal expansion F3.

### 2. Diagnosis via Obligate Absence Rather Than Positive Markers
Because *SMAD2* displays reduced penetrance of canonical LDS craniofacial features (e.g., bifid uvula, cleft palate, craniosynostosis), picking an LDS6 patient out of a cohort of overlapping connective tissue disorder patients relies on **obligate absence** F2, F18:
- Absence of **ectopia lentis** argues against Marfan syndrome (*FBN1*) F4, F9.
- Absence of **craniosynostosis** and **severe intellectual disability** argues against Shprintzen-Goldberg syndrome (*SKI*) and classic LDS1/LDS2 (*TGFBR1/2*) F2.

### 3. Clinical Takeaway for Practice
Phenotype alone is **insufficient** to definitively diagnose Loeys-Dietz syndrome 6. When a patient presents with thoracic aortic aneurysm or dissection, mild-to-moderate arterial tortuosity, and an absence of ectopia lentis and craniosynostosis, high-throughput multigene panel sequencing encompassing *SMAD2*, *SMAD3*, *TGFBR1*, *TGFBR2*, *TGFB2*, *TGFB3*, and *FBN1* is strictly mandatory to establish the diagnosis and direct tailored vascular surveillance F1, F2, F3, F18.
