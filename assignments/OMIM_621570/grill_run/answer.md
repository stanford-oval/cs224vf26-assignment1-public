# Clinical Identification and Phenotypic Characterization of Cornelia de Lange Syndrome 7 (CdLS7; MAU2)

Cornelia de Lange syndrome 7 (CdLS7; OMIM #621570) is an autosomal dominant chromatinopathy caused by heterozygous pathogenic variants in *MAU2*, which encodes the auxiliary regulatory subunit of the heterodimeric NIPBL-MAU2 cohesin loading complex (F1, F7; Parenti et al., 2020, Cell Reports, S2211-1247(20)30600-8; Parenti et al., 2025, PMC12668068). Identifying an affected individual from phenotype alone presents substantial clinical hurdles due to marked variable expressivity, phenotypic convergence with other non-classical cohesinopathies, and the absence of pathognomonic structural malformations (F1, F2, F4).

---

## 1. Clinical Phenotypic Spectrum and Exact Cohort Frequencies in CdLS7

Affected individuals with *MAU2* variants present with a developmental syndrome characterized by mild-to-moderate neurodevelopmental impairment, cranial growth restriction, postnatal growth delay, and facial dysmorphisms reminiscent of, but often attenuated compared to, classical CdLS (F1, F7; Parenti et al., 2025, PMC12668068; Peng et al., 2024, DOI:10.1002/mgg3.2318).

## 2. Discriminative Diagnostic Features and Differential Diagnosis

When evaluating a patient with craniofacial and growth features suggestive of a cohesinopathy, distinguishing CdLS7 relies heavily on negative discriminators rather than positive pathognomonic dysmorphisms (F2, F3, F4).

### Comparison Against Overlapping Differential Disorders

1. **Classical CdLS1 (*NIPBL*-related)**:
   - *Differentiating Features*: CdLS1 frequently manifests with severe intellectual disability, profound prenatal growth failure, and severe structural limb reduction anomalies (HP:0009804; e.g., transverse limb amputation, absent ulna/radius, oligodactyly) in up to 25% of individuals (F5; Deardorff et al., 2020, NBK1104; Kline, 2018, DOI:10.1038/s41576-018-0031-0). In contrast, *MAU2* probands have a true prevalence of severe limb reductions under 5% (0/18 documented; F3, F5) and exhibit a significantly milder overall cognitive and growth phenotype (F2).
   - *Confidence of Distinction*: **High** for classical CdLS1 presentations with limb deficiencies or severe diaphragmatic defects; **Low to Moderate** when comparing against mild *NIPBL* missense presentations (F2, F3).

2. **CdLS4 (*RAD21*-related) and CdLS3 (*SMC3*-related)**:
   - *Differentiating Features*: These cohesinopathies manifest with an attenuated dysmorphic spectrum, moderate cognitive impairment, and preservation of limb anatomy, paralleling CdLS7 (F2, F4). Clinical scoring tools (including the international CdLS consensus criteria) fail to distinguish *MAU2* variants from *RAD21* or *SMC3* variants, achieving a positive predictive value of under 40% (F4; Parenti et al., 2026, DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-71177-6; GeneReviews: NBK1104).
   - *Confidence of Distinction*: **Extremely Low**. A purely clinical phenotypic distinction cannot be made reliably without molecular sequencing or DNA methylation episignature profiling (F2, F4).

3. **CdLS5 (*HDAC8*-related)**:
   - *Differentiating Features*: Congenital heart defects (HP:0001627) affect approximately 60% of *HDAC8* patients and ~35% of *NIPBL* patients (F6; Casas et al., 2017, DOI:10.1016/j.medcli.2017.03.051), whereas they occur in fewer than 25% (<5/18) of *MAU2* patients (F6; Parenti et al., 2025, PMC12668068).
   - *Confidence of Distinction*: **Moderate**.

## 3. Exclusionary and Negative Diagnostic Findings

Specific findings are consistently absent in *MAU2* probands, such that their detection provides high clinical likelihood against a diagnosis of CdLS7:

* **Severe Structural Upper Limb Reduction Deficiencies (HP:0009804)**: Transverse limb amputations, forearm aplasia, absent radius or ulna, and oligodactyly have a reported frequency of 0/18 in the Parenti et al. series and <5% across all evaluations (F3, F5; Parenti et al., 2026, DOI:10.1038/s41467-026-71177-6). Presence of these severe deficiencies strongly indicates classical *NIPBL* mutations (F2, F3).
* **Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia (HP:0000776)**: Severe diaphragmatic hernias are absent (0/18) in *MAU2* probands (F3; Selicorni et al., 2021, DOI:10.3390/genes12071075; Parenti et al., 2025, PMC12668068).

## 4. Phenotypic Correlations by Variant Type, Position, Age, and Sex

Genotype-phenotype correlations in *MAU2* remain incompletely defined due to the limited total cohort size (18 individuals across 15 distinct heterozygous variants; F1; Parenti et al., 2020; Parenti et al., 2025, PMC12668068; Peng et al., 2024, DOI:10.1002/mgg3.2318):

* **Variant Type and Mechanism**: Pathogenic alterations include both loss-of-function variants (leading to haploinsufficiency) and in-frame missense/in-frame deletion variants (e.g., an in-frame 7-amino acid deletion impairing NIPBL-MAU2 heterodimerization) (F1, F4, F7, F10). While these two variant classes generate distinct peripheral blood DNA methylation episignatures, they do not partition into distinct clinical phenotypes, both producing a continuous, overlapping spectrum of mild-to-classic CdLS features (F1, F10, F11; Parenti et al., 2025, PMC12668068).
* **Sex and Age Effects**: Pathogenic variants occur de novo in both males and females without discernible sex-specific skewing or divergence in phenotypic severity (F1; Parenti et al., 2025, PMC12668068). Microcephaly and growth delay are frequently congenital or recognizable in early infancy (F14).

## 5. Contradictory and Discrepant Findings in the Literature

Phenotypic discrepancy exists between international multi-patient cohorts and single proband descriptions:

1. **Obligate Cardinal Dysmorphisms (Synophrys and Bushy Eyebrows)**: Large cohort summaries present synophrys (HP:0000664) and thick/arched eyebrows (HP:0002553) as cardinal features of *MAU2*-related CdLS (F7, F9; Parenti et al., 2025, PMC12668068). However, Peng et al. (2024, DOI:10.1002/mgg3.2318) reported a molecularly confirmed Chinese boy with a novel *MAU2* variant who exhibited a complete absence of synophrys and normal, non-bushy eyebrows, demonstrating that these cardinal facial signs are not obligate for CdLS7 (F9).
2. **Microcephaly Universality**: Microcephaly (HP:0000252) is identified as a core feature across *MAU2* probands in Parenti et al. (F7, F8). In contrast, the proband described by Peng et al. presented with an occipitofrontal circumference within the normal range (z-score = -0.5 SD), refuting the assumption that cranial growth failure is present in 100% of affected individuals (F8, F13).

## 6. Deprioritised and Refuted Hypotheses

Several candidate diagnostic rules and clinical assumptions were evaluated and deprioritised based on grounded literature refutation or contestation:

* **Bimodal Severity Stratification by Variant Mechanism (F10 - Contested)**: Proposed that truncating/LoF variants cause classical CdLS scores whereas in-frame missense variants lead to an attenuated phenocopy. *Deprioritised/Contested*: While variants segregate into two distinct DNA methylation episignatures, clinical phenotyping demonstrates marked variable expressivity spanning mild to classical CdLS across both variant classes (F10, F11).
* **Fixed Penetrance Estimates for Eyebrow Anomalies (F9 - Contested)**: Proposed that synophrys and thick eyebrows have a fixed intermediate penetrance (50–65%) in CdLS7. *Deprioritised/Contested*: Classical CdLS cohorts show variable synophrys rates across ethnic backgrounds (e.g., 40% in Liu et al., 2020, DOI:10.1002/mgg3.1471), and single *MAU2* case reports document complete absence of both features (Peng et al., 2024), invalidating rigid intermediate penetrance estimates (F9).
* **Variant-Restricted Facial Dysmorphisms (F11 - Refuted)**: Proposed that synophrys, arched eyebrows, and long eyelashes segregate selectively with truncating variants over in-frame variants. *Deprioritised/Refuted*: Literature does not demonstrate statistically significant clinical segregation of facial features by variant class (F11).
* **High-Frequency Distal Digital Anomalies (F12 - Refuted)**: Proposed that minor distal digital anomalies (HP:0004279, HP:0004209, HP:0000954) occur in >=60% of probands. *Deprioritised/Refuted*: Available published cohort texts lack exact numerators and denominators supporting this specific prevalence cutoff (F12).
* **Variant-Fixed Microcephaly Severity Cutoffs (F13 - Refuted)**: Proposed that truncating variants yield OFC z-scores <= -3.0 SD compared to -1.5 to -2.5 SD for in-frame variants. *Deprioritised/Refuted*: Not supported by clinical data, with missense probands documented with OFC z-scores as high as -0.5 SD (F13; Peng et al., 2024).
* **Postnatal Progressive vs. Congenital Microcephaly (F14 - Refuted)**: Proposed that microcephaly is predominantly postnatal and normal at birth in >50%. *Deprioritised/Refuted*: Primary cohort characterizations demonstrate that microcephaly and growth delay are often present from birth (F14; Parenti et al., 2025, PMC12668068).
* **Symmetric Somatic and Cranial Growth Correlation (F15 - Refuted)**: Proposed a strict linear correlation (r >= 0.70) between OFC and somatic growth parameters. *Deprioritised/Refuted*: Empirical literature documents frequent uncoupling of cranial and somatic percentiles in *MAU2* probands (F15).
* **Low-Frequency Surgical Reflux / GI Malformations (F16 - Refuted)**: Proposed that gastrointestinal malformations or surgical GERD occur in <20% of an 18-patient series. *Deprioritised/Refuted*: The primary series evaluating GERD details only individual presentations, including severe early GERD in the index patient; systematic cohort surgical rates are unverified (F16; Parenti et al., 2020).

---

## 7. Synthesis and Cross-Cutting Diagnostic Patterns

* **Negative Weighting in Cohesinopathy Differential Algorithms**: In suspected CdLS presentations, the absence of major structural limb reductions (HP:0009804) and diaphragmatic hernias (HP:0000776) provides the highest clinical discriminative utility, effectively ruling out classical *NIPBL*-driven CdLS1 while retaining sensitivity for *MAU2*, *RAD21*, and *SMC3* variants (F2, F3, F5).
* **Biomarker Requirement for Non-Classical Cohesinopathies**: Because *MAU2*, *RAD21*, and *SMC3* yield overlapping mild phenotypes with clinical diagnostic scoring positive predictive values under 40%, unambiguous identification of CdLS7 cannot be achieved by dysmorphology alone and necessitates multi-gene next-generation sequencing or peripheral blood episignature profiling (F4).
* **Auxiliary Loader Resilience in Morphogenesis**: Cohesin loader auxiliary subunit (*MAU2*) deficiency exhibits differential organ-specific sensitivity compared to catalytic (*NIPBL*) or ring-associated subunits, resulting in relative sparing of cardiac septation and embryonic limb outgrowth programs (F3, F5, F6).
