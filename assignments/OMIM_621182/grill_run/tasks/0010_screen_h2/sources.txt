While these disorders often share phenotypic similarities, their underlying genetic causes can vary widely, making clinical diagnosis challenging.<h4>Methods</h4>In this study, we performed whole-genome sequencing (WGS) on a family having an autosomal recessive neurodevelopmental disorder.
