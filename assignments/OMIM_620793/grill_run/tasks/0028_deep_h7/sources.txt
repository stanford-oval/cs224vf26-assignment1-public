    abstract: SNURPORTIN-1, encoded by SNUPN, plays a central role in the nuclear import of spliceosomal small nuclear ribonucleoproteins. However, its physiological function remains unexplored.     abstract: <title>Abstract</title>  <p>Background  Limb-girdle muscular dystrophy autosomal recessive type 29 (LGMDR29) is an exceedingly rare genetic disorder attributed to mutations in the SNUPN gene.
